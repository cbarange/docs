# Remove Account

### **1. If the user can log in to the portal**

Go to [https://register.greennode.ai/profile?hl=en](https://register.greennode.ai/profile?hl=en) or select “Update Profile” from the menu.

On the My Profile screen, you will find the buttons “Delete Personal Data”, “Restrict Personal Data Processing”, “Object to Personal Data Processing”, and “Withdraw Consent”. Each of these actions results in your account and personally identifiable information being deleted from the GreenNode system, in accordance with applicable law.

### **2. If the user cannot log in to the portal**

The user should send a request to [support@greennode.ai](mailto:support@greennode.ai) stating that they want to cancel their account, along with the information the technical team needs to verify the account.

Once the account has been verified, the technical team will proceed to deregister the account.

You will also be kept updated on the status of your request by email.
